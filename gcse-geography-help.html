What GCSE Geography covers

  • Natural hazards: tectonic hazards, extreme weather, climate change
  • Physical landscapes in the UK: coasts and rivers, and sometimes glaciation
  • Ecosystems and rainforests, deserts or cold environments
  • Urban issues and challenges, with a case study of a major city
  • The changing economic world and development
  • Resource management, fieldwork skills and geographical skills

How StudyAI helps

  • Explains processes like coastal erosion or plate movement step by step
  • Helps you structure a 9-mark answer with a clear argument and conclusion
  • Reminds you of the detail a case study needs: places, facts, figures

Questions you could ask

  • Explain how a headland and bay form
  • Compare the causes and effects of a tectonic hazard in a rich and a poor country
  • How should I structure a 9-mark 'to what extent' answer?
  • What are the sustainable development goals and why do they matter?

Top tip: Use specific named places and numbers in case-study answers. Check which case studies your school has taught, because they differ.
