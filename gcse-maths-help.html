What GCSE Maths covers

  • Number: fractions, percentages, standard form, surds, compound interest
  • Algebra: expanding and factorising, solving equations, quadratics (factorising, the formula, completing the square), simultaneous equations, sequences
  • Ratio and proportion: direct and inverse proportion, speed, density, pressure
  • Geometry: Pythagoras, trigonometry (SOH CAH TOA, sine and cosine rules), circle theorems, vectors, transformations
  • Probability and statistics: tree diagrams, Venn diagrams, histograms, averages from tables

How StudyAI helps

  • Shows full working with every step labelled, and keeps exact values until the final step
  • Explains the method in plain English before showing the calculation
  • Gives you a similar question to practise on once you understand one

Questions you could ask

  • Solve 3x² + 7x − 6 = 0 and show each step
  • How do I know whether to use the sine rule or the cosine rule?
  • Explain how to find the nth term of a quadratic sequence
  • A tree diagram question: bag with 4 red and 6 blue counters, two taken without replacement

Top tip: Always write down the formula, substitute, then calculate. Examiners give method marks even if your final answer is wrong.
