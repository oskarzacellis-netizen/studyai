Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version: we collect as little as possible. No accounts, no ads, no selling your data. Your questions are sent to an AI provider to get an answer, and payments are handled by Stripe.

Who is responsible for your data

Grant Ellis runs StudyAI and is responsible for your personal data. Contact: studyaihelpportal@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

  • Your questions and the conversation: sent to our AI provider, Groq, to generate an answer. We don't create an account for you or store your conversations in a database. Please don't type personal details (full name, address, phone number, passwords) into questions.
  • Two small cookies: one counts your free questions, and one remembers that you have Premium. They're essential for the service to work, so they don't need a consent banner. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Payment details: handled entirely by Stripe. We never see your card number. Stripe shares with us a subscription reference and the email you gave at checkout, so we can manage your subscription and help if something goes wrong.
  • Technical logs: our host, Vercel, keeps basic server logs (such as time of request and your IP address) for security and to keep the site running.

Who we share data with

  • Groq: processes your questions to produce answers.
  • Stripe: processes subscription payments.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.

These providers may process data outside the UK. They have their own privacy policies and safeguards. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Young users

StudyAI is made for GCSE students, many of whom are under 18. We keep data collection to the minimum needed. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before using StudyAI. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before subscribing.

How long we keep things

The two cookies last up to a year (free-question count) or until your subscription ends (Premium). Payment and subscription records are kept by Stripe and by us for as long as the law requires for accounting. Email us if you want something removed sooner.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it's used. Email studyaihelpportal@gmail.com. If you're not happy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top.
