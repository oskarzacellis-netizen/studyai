GCSE Science covers Biology, Chemistry and Physics, whether you take them separately or as Combined Science. StudyAI explains ideas simply and helps with calculations and required practicals.

What GCSE Science covers

  • Biology: cells, organisation, infection and response, bioenergetics, homeostasis, inheritance and evolution, ecology
  • Chemistry: atomic structure, bonding, the periodic table, quantitative chemistry, rates of reaction, organic chemistry, electrolysis
  • Physics: energy, electricity, particle model, forces, waves, magnetism, radioactivity, space
  • Required practicals: method, variables, results, evaluation
  • Calculations: moles, speed and acceleration, power, density, and using equations correctly

How StudyAI helps

  • Breaks complicated processes like photosynthesis and respiration into steps
  • Walks through calculations with units and the equation used
  • Helps you explain answers using the key words examiners look for

Questions you could ask

  • Explain photosynthesis and the factors that limit its rate
  • How do I balance this equation: Fe + O₂ → Fe₂O₃?
  • What is the difference between speed and velocity?
  • Describe the method for the required practical on specific heat capacity

Top tip: In 6-mark questions, learn the key terms and link ideas with 'because' and 'therefore'. Check your exam board's specification to see exactly what you need to know.
