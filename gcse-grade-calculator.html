How it works

Your marks are added up and turned into a percentage of the total marks available. That percentage is compared with the boundary for each grade. If you haven't sat every paper yet, the calculator shows how many marks you still need for each grade.

This is an estimate, not an official result. Real boundaries are only published after the exams.
