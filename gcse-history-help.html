📜 GCSE History Homework Help

Step-by-step help for GCSE students

GCSE History is about explaining why things happened and judging sources and interpretations. StudyAI helps you understand the events and practise the skills that earn marks.

Ask a History question — 10 free

What GCSE History covers

  • Your period study, thematic study, wider-world depth study and British depth study, which differ by school and exam board
  • Source questions: usefulness, content, provenance (who, why, when)
  • Interpretations: why they differ and how convincing they are
  • 'Explain why' and 'how far do you agree' longer answers
  • Building a timeline and understanding cause, consequence and significance

How StudyAI helps

  • Explains events clearly and what caused them
  • Shows how to answer a 'how useful is this source' question using content and provenance
  • Helps you plan 16-mark answers with a balanced argument and a judgement

Questions you could ask

  • How do I answer a 'how useful is this source' question?
  • What were the main causes of the First World War?
  • Explain how to structure a 'how far do you agree' essay
  • What is the difference between cause and consequence?

Top tip: Always finish longer answers with a judgement that answers the exact question, and back it with specific facts.
