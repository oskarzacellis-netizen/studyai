What GCSE English covers

  • Literature set texts: Macbeth, Romeo and Juliet, A Christmas Carol, An Inspector Calls, Jekyll and Hyde, and the poetry anthology
  • Unseen poetry and comparing poems
  • Language Paper 1: reading a fiction extract and creative writing
  • Language Paper 2: comparing non-fiction viewpoints and writing to argue or persuade
  • Analysing language, structure and form, and using context well

How StudyAI helps

  • Explains themes, characters and quotes, and what they show about the writer's ideas
  • Shows how to structure a paragraph: Point, Evidence, Explanation
  • Gives feedback on your own paragraph so you improve it rather than replace it

Questions you could ask

  • What does the dagger speech show about Macbeth's state of mind?
  • How do I compare two poems in an exam answer?
  • Explain the difference between language, structure and form
  • How can I make my descriptive writing more interesting?

Top tip: Use StudyAI to understand and plan, then write the final answer yourself. It's your own voice that earns the top marks, and your teacher will want to see it.
