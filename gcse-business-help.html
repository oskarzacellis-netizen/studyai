What GCSE Business covers

  • Business in the real world: enterprise, ownership, aims and objectives
  • Influences on business: technology, ethics, the environment, competition
  • Business operations: production, quality, customer service, suppliers
  • Finance: revenue, costs, profit, break-even, cash-flow forecasts, margins
  • Marketing: the marketing mix, market research, segmentation
  • Human resources: recruitment, training, motivation, organisation structures

How StudyAI helps

Questions you could ask

  • What is a cash flow forecast and why do businesses use one?
  • Calculate the break-even point with fixed costs of £6,000 and a contribution of £4 per unit

  • How do I write a good 9-mark business answer?
